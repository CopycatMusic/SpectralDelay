### 🎛️ What is Spectral Delay?

**CopyCat Spectral Delay** is a unique audio effect plugin that 

Break your sound into individual frequency bands and control each one independently. 

Imagine applying a long delay to only the high frequencies while keeping the bass tight and punchy. Or creating evolving feedback textures that shift across the frequency spectrum. Spectral Delay makes it all possible with an intuitive visual interface.

---

### ✨ Key Features

**🎚️ Per-Frequency Control**
- Set independent gain, delay and feedback across the spectrum

**📊 Real-Time Spectrum Display**
- See your audio's frequency content in real-time
- Switch between input and output, line graph or bar chart 

**✏️ Intuitive Curve Editor**
- Click to add control points
- Drag to shape your frequency curve
- Double-click to remove points

**⚡ Flexible Processing**
- FFT Size: 256 to 8192 (trade-off between time and frequency resolution)
- Dry/Wet mix control

**🔧 Production Ready**
- Full DAW session save/recall support
- Lightweight,clean, modern interface

### 🛠️ Installation

**macOS:**
1. Download and unzip the package
2. Copy `.vst3` to `~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3/`
3. Copy `.component` to `~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/Components/`
4. Restart your DAW and scan for new plugins

**Windows:**
1. Download and unzip the package
2. Copy `.vst3` to `C:\Program Files\Common Files\VST3\`
3. Restart your DAW and scan for new plugins
